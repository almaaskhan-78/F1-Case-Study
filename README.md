# 🏎️ F1 × Fashion

### Exploring the intersection of Formula 1, fashion, visual identity and audience culture.

This is a creative case study exploring how **Formula 1 and fashion intersect**, from visual identity and colour to driver style, team culture, and the way motorsport connects with modern audiences.

I created this project to explore a topic I'm genuinely interested in while practicing **research, visual analysis, storytelling, and presentation**.

## 🔍 What I Explored

* 🏎️ Formula 1's visual identity
* 🎨 Team colours and branding
* 👕 Driver fashion and personal style
* 📸 The relationship between motorsport and fashion
* 👥 Audience and fan culture
* ✨ How F1 has expanded beyond racing

## 🎯 Purpose

The goal of this case study was to look at Formula 1 from a perspective beyond the race itself and explore how **sport, fashion, branding and culture** come together.
